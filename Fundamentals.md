# Task 1: Cybersecurity Fundamentals

**The CIA Triad**
* **Confidentiality:** Ensures sensitive information is accessed only by authorized individuals. This is typically enforced through encryption, multi-factor authentication, and strict access controls.
* **Integrity:** Guarantees that data remains accurate and unaltered by unauthorized parties during storage or transit. This is maintained using hashing algorithms and digital signatures.
* **Availability:** Ensures information and systems are consistently accessible to authorized users when needed. This relies on hardware maintenance, backups, and DDoS protection.

**Types of Hackers**
* **Black Hat:** Malicious actors who breach networks to steal data, cause damage, or extort money. 
* **White Hat (Ethical Hackers):** Security professionals who probe systems for vulnerabilities with explicit permission from the owners to improve security defenses.
* **Gray Hat:** Individuals who exploit vulnerabilities without malicious intent, but also without permission. They often notify the system owner afterward, sometimes requesting a bounty.

**Common Threats and Their Impact**
* **Malware:** Malicious software (viruses, worms, spyware) designed to infiltrate systems. **Impact:** Causes system instability, steals private data, and can completely brick operating systems.
* **Phishing:** Deceptive communications (usually emails) disguised as legitimate entities to trick targets into revealing credentials or clicking malicious links. **Impact:** Leads to identity theft, unauthorized network access, and financial fraud.
* **Ransomware:** A specialized malware that heavily encrypts a victim's files or systems, demanding payment for the decryption key. **Impact:** Causes complete operational paralysis, permanent data loss if backups aren't available, and heavy financial damages.
